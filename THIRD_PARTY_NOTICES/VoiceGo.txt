The voice echo/interruption decision, capture-profile experiments and offline
replay tests are adapted from ideas and code in VoiceGo's liveStt.js,
voiceRecorder.js and their tests. This project retains its own AudioWorklet,
Silero/Smart Turn and ASR engines. VoiceGo's domain vocabulary and datasets
are not used as this project's ground truth.
